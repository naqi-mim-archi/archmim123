# Text 4.0 J Image-to-JSON Coverage

This is the implementation ledger for the one shared Text 4.0 J hybrid converter used by Gemini-generated and manually uploaded floorplan images. A preset is not considered supported merely because the editor can draw it; the raster extractor must have a deterministic visual signature and a regression fixture.

## J hybrid authority order

1. The unchanged J-local extractor inherited from H produces the authoritative, locally scaled wall baseline and retains its mature overlap, clutter, topology, curve, and false-wall cleanup.
2. Structured3D runs in parallel and supplies source-image wall-face candidates only. J pairs plausible parallel faces into centerline proposals, validates them against the raster, and may add only connected walls that are missing from the Local baseline.
3. J-local extraction alone owns doors, windows, openings, rooms, columns, stairs, railings, slabs, and every other non-wall architectural element.
4. Structured3D cannot select J's geometry mode or create curves. Local curve and angular-wall handling remains authoritative; Structured3D chord clusters are reference evidence only until a separately tested curve reconciler exists.
5. J-local measured or confirmed property dimensions own scale. Structured3D coordinates never determine real-world scale.
6. Every Structured3D repair is admitted transactionally. A candidate that increases Local near-overlaps or exceeds the remaining missing-wall length budget is rejected by itself; previously accepted missing walls remain in J Hybrid Final.

The middle Design Copilot pane is a read-only Structured3D candidate preview. Only the cleaned and locally scaled J hybrid result in the right pane is importable. A Structured3D failure blocks J hybrid conversion instead of silently reverting to H-style local-only wall geometry.

## Acceptance rule

New detectors must use the same `convertFloorplanImage4j` / `localImageToJSON4j` path for both image sources. They must preserve measured geometry, attach provenance/evidence, and include a representative image plus matching expected JSON before a new preset is marked supported. Width alone must never decide a visually distinct door, window, column, or stair subtype.

The portable matched regressions are exercised by `scripts/testLocalImageToJSON4j.mjs`. The original all-elements pair lives in `scripts/fixtures/text4j/allElementsRasterFixture.mjs` and `allElementsExpected.json`. The six-plan DS4 set (005-010) is stored as grayscale-compressed raster data in `dataset4RasterFixtures.mjs`; it includes five conventional plans plus a conservatively gated disconnected element sheet covering all door families, windows, wall openings, columns, stairs, and balcony rails. DS05 plans 011-013 are stored in `dataset5RasterFixtures.mjs` with captured local-OCR observations in `dataset5OcrFixtures.mjs`; they lock the reported false-column, wall-clutter, oversized-door, window-as-folding-door, and balcony-railing regressions. Inline synthetic G-only fixtures lock angular, curved, hybrid, angled hosted-opening, complete native-arc metadata, clean collinear angular hosts, canonical 45- and 30/60-degree alignment, micro-connector collapse, axis/freeform host absorption, and raster-supported mixed-junction or longer host completion; negative controls keep unsupported gaps, free angles, and sharp corners intact and prove orthogonal/curved modes are unchanged. DS09 plan 021 is the manual circular-plan acceptance case; its two strongly supported exterior runs consolidate into native arcs. DS09 angular plan 020 remains entirely chord-based, while its final representation pass reduces duplicate/fragmented chords without changing its detected doors, windows, openings, or spaces. DS09 angular-45 plan 031 locally drops two redundant wall representations and reduces loose endpoints from four to two without changing its two doors, three windows, two wall openings, or three recovered spaces. The inline weak-ink swing fixture uses grayscale 190 sampled from the reported direct-upload raster and pairs it with a leaf-only negative control. The near-closed swing fixture pairs a parallel leaf and arc over continuous host-wall ink with an otherwise identical no-arc negative control.

## Progressive conversion contract

1. Start Structured3D conversion and the unchanged J-local extraction together from the same source raster.
2. Show the unscaled Structured3D wall-face candidates in the read-only middle pane as soon as they arrive.
3. Let Local independently determine scale, geometry mode, cleaned wall topology, curves, openings, spaces, and every non-wall element.
4. Pair and raster-validate Structured3D wall faces after Local completes, then reject existing, disconnected, unsupported, or geometry-mode-conflicting centerlines.
5. Add accepted missing walls over the Local baseline one at a time. Reject only the candidate that fails the cumulative wall-length or near-overlap invariant, preserving earlier valid repairs.
6. Show the locally refined preview with temporary `Space 01`, `Space 02`, and so on while OCR completes.
7. Keep Import disabled while local OCR and final classification are processing.
8. Replace temporary labels with reliable OCR labels and unlock only the right-hand J hybrid result when final digitization completes or OCR definitively reports unavailable.
9. Treat the completed J hybrid preview as authoritative during import: do not relocate railings/slabs, convert observed windows into assumed doors, or add semantic design repairs after extraction.
