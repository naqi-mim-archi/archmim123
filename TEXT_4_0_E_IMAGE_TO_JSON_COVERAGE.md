# Text 4.0 E Image-to-JSON Coverage

This is the implementation ledger for the one shared Text 4.0 E converter used by Gemini-generated and manually uploaded floorplan images. A preset is not considered supported merely because the editor can draw it; the raster extractor must have a deterministic visual signature and a regression fixture.

## Architectural element catalog

| Element | Editor presets / subtypes | Current raster digitization | Fixture needed before expanding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Walls | Residential exterior 9 in; commercial exterior 12 in; lightweight exterior 6 in; structural interior 6 in; residential/commercial interior 4.5 in; partition 3 in; glass partition | Exterior and interior wall axes, topology, and outer-face property extents are detected. Text-cleaned tracing can recover high-density wall runs on established axes, while evidenced openings remain gaps in the raster and continuous hosts in editor JSON. Collinear overlapping/touching fragments are canonicalized without bridging an unevidenced opening; short intersection clutter and filled-block edges wholly inside an evidenced corner column are removed. A freestanding internal room box survives main-network clutter rejection only when four structural raster sides coherently enclose a reliable OCR room tag. | Structural, lightweight, partition, and glass wall examples with matching JSON are required to distinguish every subtype visually. |
| Doors | Single bath 2 ft 3 in; single bedroom 2 ft 9 in; single office 3 ft; main entrance 4 ft; double 5 ft; double main 6 ft; sliding; folding; glass | Single swing hand/facing, double swing, staggered sliding panels, and folding chevrons are detected from raster evidence. A jamb-centred quarter-circle can retain a swing door when its straight leaf is faint, without being allowed to repair wall topology by itself. A door-only weak-ink mask also retains the reported light-gray drafting style, but only for a narrow gap with one centered thin leaf and an agreeing jamb-centred quarter-circle; that mask is never used for walls, gaps, scale, rooms, windows, or other elements. A separate near-closed safeguard may propose a gap across continuous wall ink only when a two-or-more-pixel parallel leaf, hinge connector, quarter-circle arc, plausible leaf-to-wall proportions, and wall continuation beyond both jambs all agree. For narrow uploaded gaps only, a wall-band endpoint offset is accepted when the same offset contains both a thin leaf and its matching quarter-circle; width or a bare gap cannot activate that recovery. Swing leaves must be thin strokes rather than wall/fixture bands; overlapping duplicate candidates are reduced to the strongest evidence; conservative family-specific width limits reject implausibly large doors; and fixture-width parallel strokes below 0.8 m cannot become sliding doors. A folding candidate loses to a strong parallel window frame. | Glass doors and visually different folding/sliding symbols still require matched pairs before enabling more signatures. |
| Windows | Regular 3x4 ft, 4x4 ft, 5x4 ft; living 6x4 ft; angled bay; box bay; curved bay | Standard facade windows and measured clear width are detected. Hollow multi-line frames that previously merged into a solid wall are recovered without consulting OCR-covered pixels, and their jamb-side wall bands become one continuous editor host. Width alone never promotes a window to a sliding or bay subtype. | Sliding-window and angled/box/curved bay pairs are required for subtype recognition. |
| Columns | Square 9, 12, 18, and 24 in; round 12 in | Only visually filled blocks centered on the wall-axis intersection resolve to square columns. The matched four corner blocks and two isolated 18 in sample-sheet blocks remain supported; ordinary exterior corners and offset L/T junctions are rejected. | Square 9, 12, and 24 in plus round 12 in fixtures are still required. |
| Stairs | Linear; L-shape; U-shape; spiral | Repeated parallel treads recover every spatially distinct matched linear stair, including both orientations on the DS4 element sheet; step count and axis direction remain raster-derived. | L-shape, U-shape, spiral, and alternative direction-marker fixtures are still required. |
| Railings | Balcony; stair | A long external hairline with two evidenced slab connectors recovers three-sided balcony railings. A spatially nearby `BALCONY` OCR tag may recover one embedded edge or complete a connected three-edge projection; without an open-space tag, the projection must also have usable physical depth relative to the wall frame. Detached exterior dimension strings and their extension lines are rejected. An already-matched projected balcony keeps its genuine adjacent window. | Stair railings and additional embedded/recessed balcony fixtures are still required. |
| Floors/slabs | Floor boundary | Property dimensions use extreme fixed-property faces, while the single native floor slab stops on wall/column centerlines and includes the matched open balcony projection bounded by its three railings. | Irregular footprints, courtyards, multiple projections, and multi-slab examples. |
| Rooms/spaces | Room markers and labels | Enclosed spaces are detected immediately; temporary `Space 01` labels are shown while local OCR runs, then replaced by OCR labels. ## Acceptance rule

New detectors must use the same `convertFloorplanImage4e` / `localImageToJSON4e` path for both image sources. They must preserve measured geometry, attach provenance/evidence, and include a representative image plus matching expected JSON before a new preset is marked supported. Width alone must never decide a visually distinct door, window, column, or stair subtype.

The portable matched regressions are exercised by `scripts/testLocalImageToJSON4e.mjs`. The original all-elements pair lives in `scripts/fixtures/text4e/allElementsRasterFixture.mjs` and `allElementsExpected.json`. The six-plan DS4 set (005-010) is stored as grayscale-compressed raster data in `dataset4RasterFixtures.mjs`; it includes five conventional plans plus a conservatively gated disconnected element sheet covering all door families, windows, wall openings, columns, stairs, and balcony rails. DS05 plans 011-013 are stored in `dataset5RasterFixtures.mjs` with captured local-OCR observations in `dataset5OcrFixtures.mjs`; they lock the reported false-column, wall-clutter, oversized-door, window-as-folding-door, and balcony-railing regressions. The inline weak-ink swing fixture uses grayscale 190 sampled from the reported direct-upload raster and pairs it with a leaf-only negative control. The near-closed swing fixture pairs a parallel leaf and arc over continuous host-wall ink with an otherwise identical no-arc negative control.

## Progressive conversion contract

1. Decode the raster once.
2. Trace walls, wall-face extents, openings, and enclosed spaces.
3. Show that architectural preview immediately with temporary `Space 01`, `Space 02`, and so on.
4. Keep both Import actions disabled while local OCR and final classification are processing.
5. Replace temporary labels with reliable OCR labels and unlock Import when final digitization completes or OCR definitively reports unavailable.
6. Treat completed raster evidence as authoritative during import: do not relocate railings/slabs, convert observed windows into assumed doors, or add other semantic design repairs after extraction.
